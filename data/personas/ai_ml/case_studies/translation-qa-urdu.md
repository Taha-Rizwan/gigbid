# Reviewing machine-translated Urdu product copy

### The client and the problem
The client was a mid‑size e‑commerce retailer expanding into South Asian markets. Their product catalogue had been auto‑translated from English to Urdu using a generic machine‑translation service. The resulting copy was riddled with literal translations, missing diacritics and occasional nonsensical phrases, which led to confused shoppers and a noticeable uptick in customer‑service tickets.

Where the machine output was irredeemably wrong, I rewrote the phrase entirely, drawing on publicly available Urdu glossaries.
