# Updating a Wix site for a yoga studio

### The problem

I am primarily a data analyst, so web design is not my usual territory. However, a good friend who runs a small local yoga studio asked for a favour. Her website, built on Wix, had become a bit of a mess. The class timetable was outdated, and the booking links often led to dead ends or confusing pop-ups. New students were frustrated, and she was spending too much time answering emails about availability. She needed a quick, practical fix rather than a full redesign.

### What I did

I approached this as a simple content management task, similar to cleaning up a messy spreadsheet. I spent a few hours auditing the site to map out where the information lived. I then updated the core timetable with the current class schedule, ensuring the days of the week and times were accurate. I replaced the broken buttons with direct links to her booking platform, making the path from 'seeing a class' to 'booking a spot' as short as possible. I also tidied up the text on the homepage to be clearer and more inviting, using plain English that resonated with her audience. I used the basic drag-and-drop tools in Wix to rearrange the sections, ensuring the layout looked tidy on both desktop and mobile. It was not a complex engineering job, just careful attention to detail and a focus on user experience.

### Results


The updated site launched in January 2023. The new timetable is easy to read, and the booking links work seamlessly. My friend reported that the volume of 'where do I sign up?' emails dropped significantly within the first week. The studio now has a professional, clean look that matches the calm vibe of the classes. It was a small favour, but it brought her a lot of peace of mind. While this project sits outside my main niche of analytics and SQL, it was a nice change of pace and a reminder that clear, simple interfaces are just as important as complex data pipelines.
