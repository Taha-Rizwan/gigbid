# Paid-ads report for a small agency

### The problem
A boutique marketing agency managing roughly ten client accounts was struggling with the sheer volume of manual reporting required every week. Their lead analyst was copying and pasting data from various ad platforms into large spreadsheets, a process that took several hours each day. This not only ate up billable time but also introduced a risk of human error, meaning client-facing reports were often delayed or slightly inconsistent. They needed a way to present clear, reliable insights without the heavy lifting of manual data entry.

### What I did
I sat down with the agency’s head of operations to map out exactly which metrics mattered most for each client. We agreed on a core set of key performance indicators, including cost per acquisition, return on ad spend, and click-through rates. Instead of building a complex data warehouse, I designed a lightweight solution using a single, reliable tool to pull the data directly from their ad accounts. I structured the data into a clean, easy-to-read format and built a dashboard that organised the information by client and campaign. The interface was designed to be intuitive, using clear labels and simple charts so that team members without a technical background could interpret the results at a glance. I also set up an automated schedule to refresh the data every morning, ensuring that the numbers were always current before the team started their day. Throughout the process, I prioritised plain English explanations in the dashboard’s tooltips and summary sections, avoiding jargon that might confuse non-technical stakeholders.

### Results
The dashboard is now fully operational and auto-refreshes daily, removing the need for manual data collection. The agency’s team no longer spends hours on copy-paste tasks, allowing them to focus on strategy and client communication. Initial feedback has been positive, with the head of operations noting that the consistent format has made it easier to spot trends and share updates with clients. The reduction in manual work has also minimised the risk of errors, giving the team greater confidence in the accuracy of the data they present. This simple, practical solution has streamlined their workflow and improved the overall quality of their client reporting.
