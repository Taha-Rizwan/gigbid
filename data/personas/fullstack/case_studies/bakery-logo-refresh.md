# Logo refresh for a neighbourhood bakery

**Who they were**

The client was a small, family‑run bakery on a quiet neighborhood street. They’d been using the same hand‑drawn logo for years, which looked fine on paper but faded badly on their outdoor sign and the cardboard boxes they used for take‑out orders.

**What was hurting**

Customers often mentioned the signage was hard to read from the street, and the outdated look made the bakery blend in with other shops instead of standing out. The owner wanted a fresh look but didn’t have the budget for a full rebrand.

**How we fixed it**

I took a quick look at the existing logo, sketched a few concepts, and settled on a clean, simple mark that kept the original hand‑drawn feel but added a modern twist. I used **Adobe Illustrator** to digitize the design, created a version that works in both color and monochrome, and supplied the files in the formats needed for the sign maker and the box printer.

**What changed**

The new logo was printed on the shop’s exterior sign and on the bakery boxes that go out with every order. The owner reported that passersby now notice the sign more easily, and customers have started commenting on the fresh look. Sales of the bakery’s signature pastries have risen modestly, and the owner feels more confident promoting the business online.

It was a one‑off favor outside my usual SaaS and dashboard work, but I enjoyed the chance to help a local business look its best.
